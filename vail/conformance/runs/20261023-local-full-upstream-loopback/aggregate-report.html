Policy: three fresh repetitions per engine for the full suite; a validation-only selection may use fewer and is identified by provenance. Ranges describe observed samples, not confidence intervals.

Wall times are per fresh query worker and exclude startup/fixture preparation. CPU seconds sum user+system counters across the worker process tree, including Spark JVM descendants. Process CPU is sampled at 10 Hz and is a lower bound for very short-lived children. Hail/Vail ratios are greater than 1 when Hail uses more time or CPU. Geomeans are over per-case ratios; total ratios divide summed times. Cases without both engines passing are excluded from paired ratios and retained in attempts.csv and nonpasses.json.
